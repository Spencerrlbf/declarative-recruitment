06 Process

Role intake to qualified candidate slate.

Federal technical hiring succeeds when you have a partner who understands the complexities of your roles, contract requirements, and program constraints. Our specialized process accelerates hiring and delivers fully qualified, cleared candidates your team can trust from day one.

Step 01
Program and Role Intake
We clarify technical requirements, labor category, clearance needs, citizenship, work location, compensation range, engagement model, and hiring timeline.
OutputSearch profile and sourcing strategy.
Step 02
Targeted Technical Sourcing
We build a focused search across AI/ML, software engineering, data, cloud, infrastructure, DevOps, and federal-adjacent talent networks.
OutputTargeted outreach and active pipeline.
Step 03
Clearance-Aware Screening
We screen for clearance status and eligibility, citizenship, work authorization, geography, availability, compensation, and interest in federal mission work.
OutputCandidates filtered for program fit.
Step 04
Qualified Candidate Slate
You receive a concise slate with relevant background, availability, compensation alignment, location fit, and screening notes — typically within 5–10 business days of intake.
OutputActionable candidates, not volume.
Step 05
Interview, Feedback, and Close
We coordinate candidate communication, interview flow, feedback loops, offer process, and onboarding handoff.
OutputQualified candidate to accepted offer.
